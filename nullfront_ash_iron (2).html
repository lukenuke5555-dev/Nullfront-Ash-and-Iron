Field Manual — Official Game Rules

1. Setup & Mulligan

Each player deploys with a 30-card faction deck and draws 5 cards. If your opening hand has no Unit cards, it is automatically revealed, reshuffled, and redrawn. If you have at least 1 Unit, you may optionally take a hidden Mulligan once before your first action.

2. Victory & Elimination

First commander to reach 5 points wins. You score 1 point whenever you destroy an enemy unit in combat or via direct damage effects. Additionally, if you end your turn with 0 units in play, you immediately lose!

3. Turn Economy (Up to 3 Actions)

Take up to 3 distinct actions per turn: Play a Unit (Left, Center, or Right), Play a Tactical, Equip an Equipment, Attack, or Discard up to 3 cards. You cannot repeat an action category in the same turn unless a card grants an extra free action. At the end of your turn, draw until you hold 5 cards.

4. Two-Step Dice Combat

Step 1 (Hit Check): Attacker rolls Accuracy vs. Defender Evasion. Strictly higher hits.
Step 2 (Damage Check): Attacker rolls Damage vs. Defender Defense. Strictly higher destroys the defender (and its attached equipment) and awards 1 point.
