Field Manual — Official Game Rules

1. Setup & Mulligan

Each player uses a 30-card faction deck (max 2 of any card) and draws 5 cards. If your opening hand has no Unit cards, it is automatically revealed, reshuffled, and redrawn. If you have at least 1 Unit, you may optionally take a hidden Mulligan once before your first turn.

2. Winning & Losing

First player to reach 5 points wins. You score 1 point whenever your unit destroys an enemy unit in combat or with direct damage effects. Additionally, if you end your turn with 0 units in play, you immediately lose!

3. Your Turn (Up to 3 Actions)

Take up to 3 different actions per turn: Play a Unit, Play a Tactical, Equip an Equipment, Attack, or Discard up to 3 cards. You cannot repeat the same action type in one turn unless a card grants an extra free action. At the end of your turn, draw until you have 5 cards.

4. Two-Step Dice Combat

Step 1 (Hit Check): Attacker rolls Accuracy vs. Defender Evasion. If Accuracy is strictly higher, it hits.
Step 2 (Damage Check): Attacker rolls Damage vs. Defender Defense. If Damage is strictly higher, the defender (and its attached equipment) is discarded and the attacker scores 1 point.
