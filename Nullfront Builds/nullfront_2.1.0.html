Field Manual — Official Game Rules

1. Setup & Mulligan

Frontlines are Neutral cards limited to one copy of each per deck. Playing a Frontline is its own action type and uses one of your three actions. One shared Frontline can be active at a time; playing another discards the old one to its owner. Frontline effects apply universally to all players in a match. Intercept cannot target a Frontline. The Vanguard Jet can never carry Equipment; Battlefield Scavenger cannot have Equipment played directly onto it, but can receive it through indirect effects.

Each player deploys with a 30-card faction deck and draws 5 cards. If your opening hand has no Unit cards, it is automatically revealed, reshuffled, and redrawn. If you have at least 1 Unit, you may optionally take a hidden Mulligan once before your first action.

2. Victory & Elimination

Five counted deaths eliminates a commander. The last active commander wins. If all remaining commanders are eliminated by the same simultaneous effect, the match is a draw. Destroyed units increase their owner's death count; kills and self-kills are separately tracked for the final scoreboard. Nuke and card returns do not count as deaths. In Free-for-All, ending your turn with no units also eliminates you. Team Battle offers shared seven-death pools or individual limits; shared mode only loses to no units when the entire team has no units at the end of a teammate's turn.

A player who ends their turn with zero units is immediately eliminated, including AI and online players. This does not add five deaths or award kills.

When someone is eliminated, their cards leave the match, including Equipment another player scavenged. Resolve simultaneous damage using the battlefield before removing an eliminated owner's Equipment. Lingering Gas continues after its caster is eliminated. Eliminated players may spectate, but cannot act or Intercept.

3. Turn Economy (Up to 3 Actions)

Take up to 3 distinct actions per turn: Play a Unit (Left, Center, or Right), Play a Tactical, Equip an Equipment, Play a Frontline, Attack, or Discard up to 3 cards. You cannot repeat an action category in the same turn unless a card grants an extra free action. At the end of your turn, draw until you hold 5 cards.

4. Two-Step Dice Combat

Step 1 (Hit Check): Attacker rolls Accuracy vs. Defender Evasion. Strictly higher hits.
Step 2 (Damage Check): Attacker rolls Damage vs. Defender Defense. Strictly higher destroys the defender and adds one counted death to that unit's controller. Equipment goes to its owner unless a card effect salvages it.

5. Multiplayer & Intercept

Choose 2–4 players. In 3–4-player matches use the opponent tabs to view or target another battlefield. You can switch tabs at any time; the active player's tab is automatically selected at the start of their turn. Players are eliminated, not removed from turn order: their turns are skipped. All opponents means every opposing commander; adjacency never crosses separate three-slot boards.

When a Tactical is played, the other surviving commanders each receive an Intercept-or-Pass window in turn order. Playing Intercept starts a nested response cycle; once resolved, the interrupted cycle resumes at its previous position. A successfully countered Intercept no longer receives responses. Intercept cannot counter Frontline plays.

6. Rooms, AI & Rematches

Solo play supports 1–3 AI opponents. Online rooms support two to four seats filled by humans and/or host-controlled AI. The host can edit the room setup until clicking Ready; all remaining humans then Ready to begin. AI participants do not need to Ready. Results show deaths, kills by victim, self-kills and placement. Rematches return to the lobby; missing human seats can be filled with AI.

Unexpected guest disconnection pauses an online match for up to two minutes. A planned host departure can transfer host control; unexpected host loss closes the room for this version.
